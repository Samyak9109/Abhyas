## 3. Provenance and Evidence Retention

Every company pack item adheres to strict provenance standards:
- All factual claims retain `sources` containing the exact URL, title, and ISO 8601 retrieval date.
- Pack users must not strip evidence fields or claim authorship of the cited source materials.
- When exporting or sharing packs, the `retrievedAt` and `ttlDays` fields must be preserved to alert users when data is stale.
